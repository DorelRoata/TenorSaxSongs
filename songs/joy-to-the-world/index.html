5-Piece B♭ Quintet Lineup (All in Written F Major)

Every player in this ensemble reads in Written F Major (1 flat: B♭) in Treble Clef, sounding together in Concert E♭ Major:
• Soprano Sax (Best Player): Continuous Lead Melody + [A] Lyrical Solo (bars 11–15) + High A5–G5–F5 Coda descant.
• Trumpet (Intermediate): Doubles the bold opening fanfare (bars 1–2 & 16–17), then plays comfortable C4–C5 upper harmony, [A] eighth-note pulse, and a safe C5 Coda finale.
• Clarinet 1 (More Advanced): Counter-melody, 16th-note fanfare pickups (D5–E5–F5), 3rd-harmony duet in [A], and D♭5–E♭5–F5 Coda climb.
• Clarinet 2 (More Advanced): Agile running eighth-note cascades (bars 4 & 19), dotted-eighth/sixteenth figures, arpeggiated pickups (A4–C5–F5), and inner harmony.
• Tenor Sax (Intermediate): Anchors the ensemble as the true Bass & Baritone foundation in a smooth written C4–D5 range (sounding 1 octave below the other B♭ instruments).

Rehearsal Landmarks & Balance Tips

In Bars 1–2 & 16–17, Soprano Sax and Trumpet unite in unison on the iconic opening fanfare while Clarinet 1 covers the 3rd harmony. At [A] (Bars 11–15), Soprano Sax and Clarinet 1 step forward in a lyrical duet in 3rds/6ths over light staccato eighth notes in Trumpet & Clarinet 2 and warm root pedals in Tenor Sax. At the Coda (Bar 24–28), Soprano Sax soars to high A5 while Trumpet anchors C5 and Tenor Sax walks down to the low F4 root.
